THE PATTERN TO PRACTISE

Change the timing. Keep the needs.

1 · GREENER PERIODS

Shift flexible uses

In this example, late morning to afternoon has lower grid intensity. Move a flexible use there and watch its modelled emissions change.

2 · RED DEMAND BARS

Spread larger loads

Running large uses together raises demand. Spread them over time. Small unattended uses can still run together.

3 · REAL HOUSEHOLD NEEDS

Keep a workable day

Keep breakfast and dinner in their periods, wash before drying, and leave time for activities that need a person. Don’t remove a need just to improve a number.

Plan the day.
Explore the possibilities.

  1. Select or drag an activity from the library.
  2. Choose Personal, Household, or Background. Longer uses span two consecutive periods. Background appliances can overlap.
  3. Put breakfast in the morning, dinner later in the day, and drying in a later slot than washing. Other activities move freely.
  4. In live mode, use the feedback to lower modelled emissions and avoid stacking high-power activities.
  5. Continue to feedback whenever you are ready, including with an unfinished plan.

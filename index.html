You’ll explore a household energy-planning prototype, complete an optional usability questionnaire, and discuss your experience. You may use an online version or use a prototype with the researcher. 02 / THE PROTOTYPE

Plan a day.
Explore the timing.

Your goal is to meet the household’s needs while exploring when electricity is used.

Using the online planner

  1. Make an initial plan. Arrange all ten activities across the day. Each of the five lanes can hold one activity at a time. Different lanes allow activities to overlap.
  2. Follow each activity’s rules. Blocks have fixed durations. Dry clothes after washing, and keep the online tutorial at 18:00–20:00.
  3. Save your first plan. Its original version stays recorded. The first round holds back outcome feedback.
  4. Revise with feedback. Explore changes to lower modelled emissions and avoid stacking high-power activities. Then finish and save.

Select an activity and click the grid, or drag it. Double-click an activity card, or press Shift+Enter on it, to choose a time and lane directly. Click any placed block to adjust it. Every column is 30 minutes. Numbers are illustrative study values.

Using the prototype with Adith instead?

Complete the activity with Adith, then choose the option that describes what you actually used. You can continue to the same questionnaire without opening the online planner.
